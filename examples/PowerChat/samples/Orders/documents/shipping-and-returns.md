# Shipping

Orders placed before **14:00** on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Standard delivery takes two to four working days; express delivery arrives the
next working day and costs **12.50**. Orders over **150** ship free by
standard delivery.

## Order status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NEW | Received, not yet picked |
| SHIPPED | Handed to the carrier; tracking sent by e-mail |
| DELIVERED | The carrier confirmed delivery |
| RETURNED | Sent back and refunded |
| CANCELLED | Cancelled before dispatch |

# Returns

Customers may return any unused item within **30 days** of delivery for a full
refund. The refund is made to the original payment method within five working
days of the return arriving. Customised items cannot be returned unless faulty.
