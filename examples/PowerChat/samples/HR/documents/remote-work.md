# Remote work

Employees whose role allows it may work remotely up to **three days a week**,
agreed with their manager. Tuesdays are office days for every team, so that
meetings that need everyone can happen in person.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. A one-off allowance of
**300** covers a desk chair or similar home-office equipment, claimed through
expenses with a receipt.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's own time zone. Outside core
hours, work is organised freely, within the contracted hours.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country for more than **ten working days** a year needs
HR's approval in advance, because of tax and insurance rules.
