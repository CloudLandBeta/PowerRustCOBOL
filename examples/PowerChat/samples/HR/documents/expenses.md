# Expenses

Business expenses are claimed in the expenses tool within **30 days** of being
incurred, each with its receipt. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.

## Travel

- Trains and flights are booked through the travel desk. Economy class for
  journeys under six hours; premium economy above.
- Hotels up to **180** a night in capital cities, **130** elsewhere.
- Meals while travelling: up to **60** a day, alcohol excluded.

## Approvals

Claims up to **500** are approved by the line manager. Larger claims also need
the department head. Receipts must be legible; a photo is accepted.
