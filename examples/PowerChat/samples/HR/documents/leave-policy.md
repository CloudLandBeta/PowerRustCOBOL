# Annual leave

Every full-time employee earns **25 working days** of paid annual leave per
calendar year, accrued monthly. Part-time employees earn leave in proportion
to their contracted hours: someone working three days a week earns 15 days.

## Booking leave

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least **two weeks** before it starts,
and approved by the line manager. Requests for more than ten consecutive days
need four weeks' notice.

## Carry-over

Up to **five unused days** carry over to the next year and must be taken by
31 March. Any other unused leave lapses, unless a manager has recorded that
work prevented it being taken.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are paid in addition to annual leave. An employee required to
work on a public holiday receives a replacement day off within three months.

# Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager before 10:00 on the first day. From the
fourth consecutive day, a medical certificate is required. Sick days do not
reduce annual leave.
