# Confidentiality agreements (NDAs)

An NDA is required **before** any confidential information is shared with a
supplier, partner or candidate — product plans, prices, customer data or code.

## Which template

- **Mutual NDA** when both sides will share information, e.g. a joint project.
- **One-way NDA** when only the company shares, e.g. with a supplier quoting
  for work.

Both templates are in the legal library. Changes to a template's wording must
be approved by the legal team; filling in names and dates does not.

## Duration

Standard NDAs protect information for **three years** from signature. Trade
secrets are protected for as long as they remain secret.

## Signing

NDAs are signed by a director or by the legal team. The signed copy is stored
in the contracts register within five working days.
