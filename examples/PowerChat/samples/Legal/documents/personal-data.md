# Personal data

Personal data is any information about an identifiable person — a customer's
name, an employee's address, an e-mail, an order history.

## Principles

- Collect only what the purpose needs, and say what the purpose is.
- Keep it only as long as that purpose lasts: customer order data **six
  years**, applicant data **six months** after the role is filled.
- Share it outside the company only under a data processing agreement.

## Requests from individuals

A person may ask to see, correct or delete their data. Forward the request to
the privacy team the **same day**; the company must answer within **one
month**.

## Incidents

Report a lost laptop, a mis-sent e-mail or any other possible data breach to
the privacy team **within 24 hours**, even if unsure it matters.
