# Contract approval

Every contract the company signs follows the same route.

| Contract value (per year) | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Up to 10,000 | Department head |
| 10,000 to 100,000 | Department head and finance |
| Over 100,000 | Department head, finance and a director |

Contracts on a non-standard template, or with unlimited liability, also need
the legal team, whatever their value.

## Timing

Allow **ten working days** for legal review. Urgent reviews are possible when
the requester explains the deadline in advance.

## Records

The signed contract, its approvals and its renewal date go into the contracts
register. The owner is reminded **90 days** before an automatic renewal.
